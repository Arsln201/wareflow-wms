# 📦 WareFlow AI
### Smart Warehouse Operations Platform

---

# 🚀 Project Goal

WareFlow AI is a warehouse management system designed to help warehouse employees quickly search product locations, generate QR labels, manage inventory, and improve warehouse operations.

Inspired by real warehouse workflows where damaged or missing QR labels slow down product picking.
